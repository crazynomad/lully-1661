# Competitor Scan — Lisbon Bakery / Brunch Landscape

**Status:** v0.1 — desk research pass
**Prepared:** 2026-04-17
**Source:** client-provided shortlist (4 competitors)
**Scope:** Lisbon bakery + brunch + specialty coffee market — direct and adjacent competitors

## TL;DR — How lully 1661 sits in the landscape

| Axis | Where lully 1661 lands |
|------|------------------------|
| **Scale** | 3 stores — **middle of the pack** (bigger than doBeco / Padaria 110; smaller than Dear Breakfast / The Folks) |
| **Category** | Bakery + brunch + café — **broadest offering** of the four; closest analogue is doBeco |
| **Positioning** | **Baroque heritage + 17th-century namesake** — unique in this set; every competitor is some flavor of "modern minimalist" |
| **Digital maturity (current)** | Lowest — no live site, social-only |
| **Digital maturity (post-launch)** | Will jump ahead of doBeco and Padaria 110; pull level with Dear Breakfast; still behind The Folks on e-commerce depth |

**Strategic insight: the modern-minimalist look is commodity in Lisbon.** Four of four competitors sit inside that aesthetic. lully 1661's Baroque-contemporary direction (copperplate engravings + gold flourish + ink signature) is the only real differentiator in this set. That is a *real* brand moat, not just a design preference.

## Competitor deep-dives

### 1. Dear Breakfast — [dearbreakfast.com](https://www.dearbreakfast.com/)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Category** | All-day brunch café (egg-centric) |
| **Locations** | **6** — Chiado, Bica, Alfama, Santos, Príncipe Real, Marvila Bakehouse |
| **Hours** | 8:00–16:00 most; Marvila Thu–Sun 11:00–22:00 |
| **Positioning** | Modern minimalist — white-washed walls, Portuguese marble, exposed brick. "Properly cooked eggs, exquisite coffee and juice." |
| **Online order / delivery** | Uber Eats delivery · reservation system built in · branded merch e-shop |
| **Price visibility** | Merch only (€12.50–€20); food menu is PDF download |
| **Signature items** | Benedict · caramel-banana pancakes · scrambled eggs with truffles |
| **Languages** | PT + EN (bilingual, PDFs in both) |
| **Platform** | **Webflow** (custom build) |

**Threat level to lully: medium-high.** They own "Lisbon all-day brunch" in digital presence and have the scale to back it. Their brunch menu at Marvila Bakehouse is *exactly* the space lully Anjos is moving into on 2026-05-15.

**What we learn:** Bilingual PT/EN with downloadable menu PDFs is table stakes. Reservation + Uber Eats is table stakes.

### 2. The Folks — [thefolks.pt](https://thefolks.pt/)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Category** | Specialty coffee + brunch + roastery |
| **Locations** | **6** — Chiado, Alfama, Sé, Blue Street, Santos, Belém |
| **Positioning** | Community + quality — "spaces where people feel at home." **World's Top 100 Coffee Shops 2025/26 (#88).** |
| **Online order / delivery** | **Full e-commerce** — coffee beans, equipment, gift cards, B2B wholesale. Worldwide shipping (free >€60). |
| **Price visibility** | Full — coffee beans €16–€30+, drip €3, gift cards €30–€250 |
| **Menu categories** | Coffee origins · equipment · merch · gift cards · brunch (visible in menu, not fully detailed) |
| **Signature positioning** | "Exceptional coffee and amazing food, every day" |
| **Languages** | PT + EN |
| **Platform** | **Shopify** (`thefolks.pt/cdn/shop/`) |

**Threat level to lully: medium.** Different primary category (coffee-first vs. bakery-first), but same geographic footprint and clearly the **benchmark for Shopify bakery/café e-commerce in Lisbon**.

**What we learn (big one):**
1. **Shopify works in this market** — this is the closest direct validation for our platform choice.
2. **The B2B wholesale pattern maps 1:1 onto lully's "Lully Inside" section.** Their page structure is worth a closer look.
3. **Gift cards + worldwide shipping** — both in our Phase 2 scope. We should sanity-check our v1.1 cutover against how The Folks handles these.

## Implications for the SOW / build

1. **Shopify is the right platform.** The Folks' success on Shopify (ranked world top-100) is the closest benchmark. No Webflow/WordPress justification needed.
2. **PDF menu downloads** (à la Dear Breakfast) are a low-cost table-stakes addition — worth adding to v1.0 if client has menu PDFs ready.
3. **Uber Eats delivery** was flagged Phase 2 in our SOW. Given Dear Breakfast has it, we should keep it in Phase 2 but mention in the SOW discussion that it's a known gap vs. direct competitors.
4. **B2B wholesale page pattern** — The Folks has a mature version; we should study it before finalizing the Lully Inside page structure.
5. **Brand differentiation is already in hand.** Four competitors, four variants of modern minimalism. The Baroque-contemporary visual direction is genuinely the clear lane.
6. **Anjos flagship positioning** — lean hard into "Anjos = the brunch room." doBeco has no single flagship; Dear Breakfast has 6 equal stores; the Anjos-as-flagship story is differentiated.
